---
title: Named sections
description: Present maintenance fallbacks for part of a page.
---

```vue
<NuxtMaintenance section="checkout">
  <Checkout />
  <template #fallback="{ message }">
    <CheckoutUnavailable :message="message" />
  </template>
</NuxtMaintenance>
```

The default slot renders while up. The fallback receives `section`, `message`, and `since`. Without it, the component renders an unstyled accessible status message. State resolves during SSR, enters the Nuxt payload, and refreshes on navigation; v1 has no polling or push.

Section maintenance is presentation control, not authorization. Continue enforcing business rules on the server.
